Interactive guitar tool

Fretboard.js

A dependency-free plugin that draws a guitar fretboard in SVG and labels every note on it. Change the tuning or fret count below, or highlight a scale to see it laid out across the neck.

SVG · dependency-free
Root notes of a highlighted scale are shown in brass; other scale tones share a warmer wood tone. "Fret taper" controls how much the frets narrow toward the body — 0 keeps them equally spaced, 1 matches a real guitar neck, and higher values exaggerate the effect further. Include fretboard.js and fretboard.css in your own page, then call new Fretboard(container, options) — see the source comments for the full option list.
